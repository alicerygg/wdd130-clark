About This Tutorial

In this tutorial we will be going in depth in a step by step process teaching you how to create kerbal space program animations in blender. A basic understanding of blender, as well as owning a copy of KSP on your computer, is necessary for this tutorial.

Watch the video version here: Video

Blender Basics

We will be working with Blender 3.6 or earlier. It is necessary to import ships and parts in this version of Blender, as the importer addon will not work in other versions. We will also need to download the io_object_mu addon from GitHub.
